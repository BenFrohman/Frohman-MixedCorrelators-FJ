**Frohman problem.** Let F = W ⊕ W ⊕ W with W = u^5 v + v^6, and let J act by ζ_6 on every coordinate. Compute the genus-zero primary FJRW three-point correlators of (F, ⟨J⟩) on mixed broad–narrow and mixed-block insertions.

**Guéré’s theorem (not this problem).** For a *single* chain polynomial and any admissible G, the Hodge-capped Polishchuk–Vaintrob class on (W,G)-spin moduli is given by a t-limit of Chern characters of the spin line bundles (arXiv:1509.07047).

**Relation.** Guéré is the tool for each atom. The problem is the three-block sum.
