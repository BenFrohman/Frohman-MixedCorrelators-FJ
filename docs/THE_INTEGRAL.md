$$
\langle\alpha,\beta,\gamma\rangle_{0,3}^{F,\langle J\rangle}
=
\int_{\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,3}^{F,\langle J\rangle}}
\bigl[\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,3}^{F,\langle J\rangle}\bigr]^{\mathrm{vir}}.
$$

That is the Frohman mixed three-point. It is a definition, not a number.

## Each symbol

- \(F = W \oplus W \oplus W\), \(W = u^5 v + v^6\).
- \(J\) scales every coordinate of \(\mathbb{C}^6\) by a primitive sixth root of unity. \(\langle J\rangle \cong \mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z}\).
- \(\alpha,\beta,\gamma\) run over the 2610-dimensional FJRW state space \(\mathcal{H}_{F,\langle J\rangle} = 2605 \oplus 5\).
- Subscript \(0,3\): genus zero, three marked points.
- \(\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,3}^{F,\langle J\rangle}\) exists if and only if the decorations multiply to \(J\), i.e. \(k+\ell+m \equiv 1 \pmod{6}\).
- \([\overline{\mathcal{M}}]^{\mathrm{vir}}\) is the virtual class on that stack. The integral is the pairing of that class against 1 (primary, no \(\psi\)-classes).

## Two zeros, one OPEN room

- Fail \(k+\ell+m \equiv 1 \pmod{6}\): the stack is empty. The integral is 0 because the domain is empty. 180 of 216 ordered decoration triples.
- Pass the rule: the stack is nonempty. The integral is a number that has not been evaluated for mixed insertions. 36 rooms. OPEN.
- One pairing on the narrow Lefschetz line is known independently: \(\int_X \omega^4 = 6\). That is volume, not the mixed table.

## What does not evaluate it

Residue triples of \(\mathrm{Jac}(W)\) (values in \(\{1,-6\}\)).  
Three Guéré integrals of one chain.  
Krawitz for \(G_{\max}\).  
\(QH^*(V(F))\) (stable maps, different moduli).  
CMP zeta-generator paper (associators, not this virtual class).  
Writing 0, 1, 6, or 1/6 into a BBN / BNN / mixed-block cell.

Locked ranks remain \(\mu(W)=25\), \(\mu(W^T)=26\), \(|\det A|=30\).
