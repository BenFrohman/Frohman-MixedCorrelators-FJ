## Way 1. Evaluate

$$
\langle\alpha,\beta,\gamma\rangle_{0,3}^{F,\langle J\rangle}
=
\int_{\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,3}^{F,\langle J\rangle}}
\bigl[\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{0,3}^{F,\langle J\rangle}\bigr]^{\mathrm{vir}}
$$

on every room that passes k+ℓ+m ≡ 1 (mod 6). Thirty-six decoration rooms.

Guéré computes Hodge integrals of one chain. It does not run on F = W ⊕ W ⊕ W.

## Way 2. A reconstruction theorem for this group, not G_max

There must exist a finite seed list S of those integrals such that WDVV plus the pairing plus the selection rule determine every other genus-zero primary number of (F, ⟨J⟩).

The seeds have to come from the virtual class on F-spin moduli. Residues of Jac(W) and three Guéré integrals of W are the wrong input.

Krawitz’s axioms are written for G_max (order 27000). Here the group is ⟨J⟩ (order 6). Basalaev (arXiv:1610.07428) already notes that for some non-maximal groups the axioms reconstruct only up to scaling: particular numerical values need an extra evaluation. That is this situation, in dimension 2610 rather than 6.
