## What quantum cohomology is

Additively QH*(X) is H*(X) with Novikov coefficients. The product deforms the cup product by genus-zero Gromov–Witten three-points:

    α ⋆ β = α ∪ β + Σ_{γ, d>0} ⟨α, β, γ^∨⟩_{0,3,d}^X q^d γ.

Small QH uses only divisor / curve-class parameters. Big QH is the formal Frobenius manifold on all of H*(X). Associativity of ⋆ is WDVV. The classical cup product is the q → 0 limit.

Kontsevich–Manin reconstruction needs H*(X) generated by H^2. A fourfold fails that: H^4 is not generated by divisors. So the first reconstruction theorem does not compute QH*(X) for a sextic in P^5.

## Two A-models, two moduli spaces

| | Gromov–Witten / QH*(X) | FJRW |
|---|---|---|
| Target | variety X | singularity (W, G) |
| Moduli | M-bar_{g,n}(X, β) | M-bar_{g,n}^{W,G} |
| State space | H*(X) (or Chen–Ruan if orbifold) | FJRW state space H_{W,G} |
| 3-points | ⟨α, β, γ⟩_{0,3,β}^X | ⟨α, β, γ⟩_{0,3}^{W,G} |
| Product | quantum cup product | FJRW quantum product |
| Associativity | WDVV | WDVV |

Replace “stable maps to X” by “spin curves for a potential W.” They are not the same ring written twice.

## LG/CY bridge

When W is quasihomogeneous and the charges sum to 1 (Calabi–Yau condition), Chiodo–Ruan give a bidegree isomorphism of state spaces

    H_{W,G} ≅ H*_CR([X_W / G-bar]).

That is why 2610 = 2605 + 5 matches dim H^\bullet(X). Vector-space match. Genus-zero invariants are then related by a symplectic transformation of Givental cones (analytic continuation), proved for many hypersurfaces including the quintic, and stated as LG/CY in general.

What that correspondence does not do:

- evaluate mixed three-points of (F, ⟨J⟩) from three Gueré integrals of W;
- identify the FJRW ring of ⟨J⟩ with QH*(V(F)) by a formula on disk today;
- fill Hodge Term B. Algebraicity of a class in H^{2,2} is classical, not a quantum correction.

## What QH*(V(F)) would compute

X = V(F) ⊂ P^5 is a Calabi–Yau fourfold with h^{1,1} = 1. Small QH is generated by ω = c_1(O(1)) plus primitive H^4 plus Lefschetz duals. Genus-zero three-points with primitive (2,2) insertions are Gromov–Witten numbers of maps to X. Klemm–Pandharipande (arXiv:math/0702189) study GW of CY fourfolds, including the Fermat sextic; holomorphic anomaly conjecturally solves that host. That is curve-counting on X, not mixed FJRW of the three-chain F = W ⊕ W ⊕ W.

## Springer 10.1007/s00220-025-05489-x

Title: Canonicalizing Zeta Generators: Genus Zero and Genus One.
Authors: Dorigoni, Doroudiani, Drewitt, Hidding, Kleinschmidt, Schlotterer, Schneps, Verbeek.
Communications in Mathematical Physics, DOI 10.1007/s00220-025-05489-x.

What it is: canonical polynomials for motivic multiple zeta values, a canonical map to the f-alphabet, and canonical genus-one zeta generators acting on Enriquez elliptic associators, expanded in Tsunogai geometric derivations. Periods, Drinfeld / elliptic associators, modular graph forms.

What it is not:
- not a computation of ⟨α, β, γ⟩_{0,3}^{F,⟨J⟩};
- not QH*(V(F));
- not a Hodge miss;
- not Guéré numbers for this atom.

Contact with this lab is distant and real: associators and MZVs appear in Givental reconstruction and in genus-zero string / polylog periods. That shared language does not evaluate the 36 OPEN rooms and does not complete Term A or Term B of rational Hodge on fourfolds.
