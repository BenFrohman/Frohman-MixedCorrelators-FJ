These five rows are real numbers in *other* tables. The last column is the category error that would turn them into invented mixed correlators.

| Real table | Real numbers | What they actually are | Fake use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residue 3-points of Jac(W) | 38 triples = 1, 4 triples = −6 | Coefficient of the socle u³v⁵ in abc, after u⁵ = −6 v⁵ | Write 1 as a mixed F correlator |
| Degree / volume | 6 = ∫_X ω⁴ = deg(X ⊂ ℙ⁵) | One pairing on the Lefschetz line (narrow, top power) | Write 6 in every NNN or BNN cell |
| Off-rule vanishing | 0 | Decoration product ≠ J, or Jac degree sum ≠ 8 | Write 0 in every allowed mixed cell |
| Age / group order | fractions k/6, |⟨J⟩| = 6 | Age of J^k and the order of the cyclic group | Write 1/6 as a correlator (the 1/6 in that scratch) |
| Slot census | 3·2605² = 20 358 075, 12·2605 = 31 260, 21 | How many basis triples exist | Treat a slot count as a correlator value |
