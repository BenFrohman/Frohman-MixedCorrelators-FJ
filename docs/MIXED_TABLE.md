# Mixed correlators — the construct, not the image

Author: Benjamin Stanley Frohman. Copyright (c) 2026. Apache-2.0.

The Grok Table.pdf / IMG_0713 image is a proved/not-proved ledger.
It is not the mixed-correlator table. The construct is:

    ⟨α, β, γ⟩_{0,3}^{F, ⟨J⟩}
    = ∫ [M-bar_{0,3}^{F,⟨J⟩}]^vir

over the 2610-dimensional space, selection rule: decorations multiply to J.

## Cells that are not mixed correlators of F

- ∫_X ω^4 = 6. Volume of the sextic. Narrow-line pairing. Not mixed.
- 42 residue triples of Jac(W) against socle u^3 v^5. Algebra of one block.
- Pure-tensor product on Jac(F). Algebra, not the virtual class of F.
- Hilbert (1, 426, 1751, 426, 1). Dimension match. Not an integral.

## Cells that ARE the problem (OPEN)

- BBB mixed-block: three broad states that mix the three copies of W.
- BBN: two broad, one narrow.
- BNN: one broad, two narrow.

No number is written in those cells. 0, 1, and 6 are forbidden fills.

A later grind that evaluates those cells from the virtual class on F-spin
moduli, or from a reconstruction theorem proved for ⟨J⟩, is the solution.
This file does not invent that grind.
