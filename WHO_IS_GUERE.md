# Who is Guéré?

Author: Benjamin Stanley Frohman. © 2026. Apache-2.0.

Jérémy Guéré. Algebraic geometer. Formula for Hodge integrals in FJRW theory of chain polynomials.

Paper: *Hodge integrals in FJRW theory*, arXiv:1509.07047 (2015); Michigan Math. J. 66 (2017) 831–854.
DOI: 10.1307/mmj/1508810817.

Later related work: *Hodge–Gromov–Witten theory*, arXiv:1908.11409, all-genus Hodge-GW of chain/loop hypersurfaces.

The formula is his. The three-block mixed-correlator problem is not. Naming the tool is citation, not attribution of the new problem to him.
